# Build surface prep

- Wash the plate with dish soap and water, including new plates. Scrub textured plates in small circles, rinse well, and air dry rather than using a cloth.
- IPA is fine for routine cleaning but doesn't remove skin oils, so wash periodically. Handle plates by the edges.
- Smooth PEI: scuff it with a scouring pad or 800–1000 grit paper for adhesion. The trade-off is a less glossy bottom surface.
- Acetone is a last resort only, since it degrades PEI.
See [poor bed adhesion](../defects/poor-bed-adhesion.md).
