# FDM print defect diagnostic matrix

Use this matrix to generate competing hypotheses and one-variable tests. Community reports are useful for discovering hypotheses but remain C/D evidence; official documentation and reproducible measurements take precedence. [^simplify3d] [^ellis-guide]

| Observable symptom | First hypotheses | Discriminating test | Corrective direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gaps, weak walls, missing top lines | clog, wet filament, wrong rotation distance, flow cap, low EM | slow extrusion test; inspect filament/nozzle; compare slow vs high-flow region | fix hardware/moisture/rotation first; then cap flow or tune EM |
| Stringing/wisps | moisture, temperature, PA, leaks, retraction, travel path | dry spool comparison; temperature/retraction tower; inspect nozzle and Bowden/direct-drive play | dry, correct temperature/PA, then retraction/wipe/travel |
| Corners bulge/echo | PA too low, ringing, acceleration, loose mechanics | low-accel square; inspect spacing/orientation; compare PA tower | repair mechanics; tune input shaping/accel and PA |
| Layers shift horizontally | belt/pulley, motor current, driver heat, nozzle strike, acceleration | identify axis; watch for skips; inspect belt path and curled parts | mechanical/electrical repair, lower motion limits |
| Corners lift/warp | shrinkage, drafts, surface, bed/chamber/cooling | compare enclosed/heat-soaked and draft-controlled print | improve surface/bed/chamber and material cooling strategy |
| First layer uneven | sheet contamination, Z/sensor, gantry, thermal drift, mesh | patch across bed cold vs heat-soaked; inspect sheet and mechanics | clean/seat sheet, correct model-specific Z/mesh/thermal state |
| Blobs at starts/ends | seam, restart pressure, coasting/wipe, PA, overextrusion | observe one perimeter and classify start vs end defect | tune seam/restart/wipe/coast/PA separately |
| Rough underside/support surface | insufficient cooling, layer height, support density/interface/offset | preview feature type; vary one support parameter | lower layer height, improve cooling/interface, adjust separation |
| Bridge sag | bridge recognition/direction, flow, speed, cooling, span too long | inspect preview, verify span direction, short bridge test | high controlled cooling, bridge flow/speed, supports if needed |
| Weak interlayers | low temperature, too much cooling, high speed/flow, moisture, orientation | dry vs wet; controlled temperature/fan/speed comparison; fracture-plane inspection | material-specific thermal/cooling/flow correction |

## Mandatory context before numeric advice

Collect printer/model, firmware and version, slicer/version, nozzle diameter/material/geometry, extruder architecture, material product/color/spool/moisture, layer height/width, speed/acceleration, temperature, fan, chamber, build surface and test geometry. If unknown, state `unknown`; do not fill in a generic number.

## Test discipline

1. Confirm the symptom is repeatable.
2. Inspect non-setting causes first: moisture, clogs, leaks, belts, screws, wiring, surface and heat soak.
3. Choose the cheapest test that distinguishes the top two hypotheses.
4. Change one variable at a time and preserve the baseline G-code/profile.
5. Re-run a functional validation part, not only the calibration coupon.

## Source conflicts

- Simplify3D publishes broad direct-drive/Bowden retraction ranges, while Ellis recommends much smaller direct-drive starting values; extruder geometry and firmware retraction mode explain the difference. Keep both as scoped starting points, not a universal range. [^simplify3d] [^ellis-guide]
- Prusa and manufacturer material profiles often differ from community “best” values because printer family, nozzle, sheet, firmware and product grade differ. [^prusa-kb] [^community-corpus]
- Klipper, PrusaSlicer and OrcaSlicer may each control PA, retraction and acceleration. Determine the active authority from generated G-code and firmware configuration before changing a profile.
