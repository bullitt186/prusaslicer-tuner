# Source research archive

This directory contains raw, source-oriented research reports produced before consolidation into `knowledge/`. The reports are audit material, not the MCP's primary topic index. They preserve inspected revisions, corpus selection, claims, numerical values, conflicts and gaps.

## Coverage

Twenty-four source URLs were assigned to separate research agents. Community sources use representative corpora rather than an archive-wide scrape. See `coverage.md` for status and the consolidated topic map.

## Evidence convention

- A: manufacturer specification or reproducible measurement
- B: official technical documentation or engineering analysis
- C: repeated community observation with identifiable conditions
- D: anecdote, weakly verified recommendation or bounded inference

Community reports are retained for hypothesis generation and diagnostics, not as universal proof. Numeric values remain attached to the conditions reported by the source.
